## Getting Started

2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Complete the registration form

## User Registration & Login

### Creating an Account
1. Click the **Sign Up** button on the homepage
2. Fill in the following information:
   - **Email**: Your valid email address
   - **Password**: At least 8 characters (mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols)
   - **Full Name**: Your name
   - **Preferred Language**: Select your language

3. Accept the Terms of Service
4. Click **Create Account**
5. Check your email for a verification link
6. Click the link to activate your account

### Logging In
1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Optional: Check "Remember Me" to stay logged in
4. Click **Login**

### Resetting Your Password
1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for a password reset link
4. Click the link and create a new password
5. Log in with your new password

## Trading

### How to Buy a Stock

1. Click the **Buy** button
2. Search for the stock by symbol (e.g., AAPL) or company name
3. Select the stock from results
4. Enter the number of shares you want to buy
5. Review the total cost and fees
6. Click **Confirm Purchase**
7. Your order will be executed immediately

### How to Sell a Stock

1. Go to **My Portfolio**
2. Find the stock you want to sell
3. Click the **Sell** button next to the stock
4. Enter the number of shares to sell
5. Review the proceeds (after fees)
6. Click **Confirm Sale**
7. Your order will be executed immediately

### Understanding Trade Execution
- Orders are executed at the current market price
- A trading commission of 0.1% applies to all trades
- Trades are processed immediately during market hours
- You cannot trade stocks you don't own (no short selling)

### Order History
1. Navigate to **Trading History**
2. View all your past trades
3. Filter by date range or stock symbol
4. Export transaction report (PDF/CSV)

## Portfolio Management

### Viewing Your Portfolio

1. Click **Portfolio** in the main menu
2. View all your stock holdings with:
   - **Symbol**: Stock ticker
   - **Quantity**: Number of shares owned
   - **Average Cost**: Your average purchase price
   - **Current Price**: Today's stock price
   - **Total Value**: Current position value
   - **Gain/Loss**: Dollar amount gained/lost
   - **Gain/Loss %**: Percentage return

### Portfolio Performance Analytics

- **Daily Performance**: Today's gains/losses
- **Portfolio History**: 1D, 1W, 1M, 3M, 1Y, All-Time
- **Allocation Pie Chart**: Percentage breakdown of holdings
- **Performance Comparison**: Compare to S&P 500 index

### Dividend & Corporate Actions
- Track dividend payments received
- View stock splits and adjustments
- Corporate action notifications

### Portfolio Goals
1. Set a target portfolio value
2. Track progress toward your goal
3. View recommended adjustments
4. Get alerts when milestones are reached

## Weather Dashboard

### Weather Widget Features

The Weather Dashboard displays:
- **Current Temperature**: In Fahrenheit or Celsius
- **Weather Condition**: Sunny, Cloudy, Rainy, Snowy, etc.
- **Humidity Level**: Percentage moisture
- **Wind Speed**: Speed and direction
- **UV Index**: Sun exposure level
- **Visibility**: Distance visibility
- **Feels Like**: Perceived temperature

### Searching for Weather

1. Click the **Weather** icon on the dashboard
2. Enter a city name or zip code
3. Select from suggestions
4. View current weather for that location

### Weather Forecast

1. From any weather view, click **5-Day Forecast**
2. View weather predictions for the next 5 days
3. Plan your activities accordingly

### Weather Alerts

- Receive notifications for severe weather
- Configure alert preferences in settings
- Weather warnings displayed on dashboard

## FAQs

### Account Questions

**Q: How much virtual money do I start with?**
A: You begin with $10,000 in virtual currency for paper trading.

**Q: Can I reset my portfolio balance?**
A: Yes, contact support or reset from your account settings. Note: This will clear all trading history.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) and secure authentication (JWT tokens).

### Trading Questions

**Q: What are the trading hours?**
A: You can trade Monday-Friday, 9:30 AM - 4:00 PM EST, matching US stock market hours.

**Q: Can I trade after hours?**
A: No, after-hours trading is not available in this educational platform.

**Q: What's the commission fee?**
A: 0.1% per trade, calculated automatically.

**Q: Can I short sell stocks?**
A: No, short selling is not available. You can only sell stocks you own.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What browser should I use?**
A: Any modern browser works best: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge.

**Q: Can I use the mobile app?**
A: Yes, the platform is fully responsive on mobile devices.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes, you can export trading history and portfolio reports as PDF or CSV.

### Weather Dashboard Questions

**Q: How often is weather data updated?**
A: Weather data is updated every 30 minutes.

**Q: Which weather API is used?**
A: We use OpenWeatherMap API for accurate, real-time data.

**Q: Can I get severe weather alerts?**
A: Yes, enable notifications in your settings.
